How to Reach PCTE

By Bus

Multiple city buses connect to PCTE from all major points in Ludhiana. The nearest bus stop is Jhande Chowk.

By Train

Ludhiana Railway Station is 8 km away. Auto-rickshaws and taxis are available outside the station.

By Car

Ample parking space available. Located just off the Ludhiana-Chandigarh highway.
